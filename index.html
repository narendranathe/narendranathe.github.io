Rebuilding the pipelines to be rerunnableE1-CDC · E-IDEM

Problem

What changed

Streaming risk

Portfolio Risk Analytics

Problem
Overnight VaR means a portfolio manager who moves a position intraday has no idea what their risk looks like until the next morning.
Method
A Kafka producer streams market ticks into a partitioned topic. Spark Structured Streaming aggregates them on 5-second tumbling windows behind a 10-second watermark, a Delta writer lands the bronze layer, and a FastAPI service computes historical-simulation VaR at 95% and 99% confidence over the processed data. Streamlit sits on top.
Proof P-RISK
Historical simulation over a 30-day rolling window, chosen over a parametric model because equity returns are fat-tailed and a normal assumption understates the tail. Component VaR is computed per position, so the dashboard can say which holding is driving portfolio risk and not only what the total is.
What you can verify
Public repo. The windowing, the watermark, and the VaR maths are all readable in src/. If no processed data is present the API falls back to a seeded sample set, which is worth knowing before reading any number off a fresh checkout.
