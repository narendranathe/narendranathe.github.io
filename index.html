// career

Five companies.
One throughline.

I am currently a Data Engineer at ExponentHR in Dallas, where I have been since July 2024.

Before Dallas, there was Missouri S&T — a Master’s in Data Science, finished December 2024 with a 4.0 GPA, and a side project that quietly saved the program $3,200 a month in Azure spend by rewriting the data pipeline nobody had touched in two years. Published in Taylor & Francis the same semester.

At Zomato I learned what scale actually means — not the kind on a slide deck, but the kind where a query plan you wrote on Tuesday changes the unit economics of a city by Friday. I built the analytics that backed a 9% market share gain in a contested region. You learn quickly that infrastructure is a moral commitment: someone downstream is going to trust your numbers to make a decision they cannot take back.

Before that, C2FO taught me what enterprise data engineering looks like when money is literally moving through your pipelines, and udaan.com taught me what it looks like when the merchants on the other end of those pipelines are people you can meet. Five companies, one throughline: build the boring layer well, and the interesting layer becomes possible.
