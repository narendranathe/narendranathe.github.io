# Ubiquitous Language

Keep the naming in this repo stable across specs, implementation, copy, and portfolio discussions.

## Purpose

- `portfolio`: the recruiter-facing site and case-study surface for Narendranath Edara.
- `selected systems`: the flagship shipped systems that carry the main hiring signal on the homepage.
- `case study`: a deeper system page that expands one project beyond homepage scan depth.

## Current Terms

- `measured impact`: operational outcomes, latency, scale, onboarding speed, and quality signals.
- `architecture`: the retrieval, workflow, data, and interface structure behind a system.
- `workflow platform`: a product that connects discovery, generation, execution, and tracking across shared state.
- `reusable engine`: extracted logic that can be distributed through multiple surfaces such as CLI, MCP, PyPI, Streamlit, or APIs.
- `proof rail`: the visual-and-metric panel near the top of the homepage that combines flagship product context, employer recognition, and measurable outcomes.
- `specs/README.md`: the durable design-decision log for this repo.

## Named Systems

- `ExponentHR`: enterprise AI platform work centered on governed NL-to-SQL, semantic joins, and secure query execution.
- `AutoApply AI`: workflow platform for discover -> tailor -> apply -> track across browser, backend, and vault memory.
- `tailor-resume`: extracted tailoring engine behind the larger workflow stack.
- `JobScout`: supporting discovery and ranking engine behind the broader application flow.

## Notes

- Prefer `selected systems` over vague labels like `featured work` or `supporting trust`.
- Prefer `engineering notes` over generic `blog` or `writing` language when discussing architecture posts.
- Add or refine terms after substantive product, copy, or architecture changes.
