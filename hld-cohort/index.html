FAQ

Questions before you join.

Who is this for?

For software engineers who want to master System Design, build strong architectural thinking, and confidently tackle interviews.

Do I need prior system design knowledge?

No. Week 01 starts from foundations.

Is it suitable for experienced engineers?

Yes. From entry-level developers to senior engineers, tech leads and architects.

Are sessions live?

Yes. Every session is taught live by Dhruv, with Q&A.

What language are classes taught in?

Hinglish — a mix of English and Hindi. Diagrams and technical terms remain in English.

Will I get recordings?

Yes. Every session is recorded and shared with cohort students.

Are mock interviews included?

Yes. Mock interviews run between weeks, with a final session in week eight.

What if I miss a class?

Watch the recording and bring questions to the next live Q&A or community.

Is this for interviews or real engineering?

Both. The cohort teaches how real systems are designed and how to communicate that thinking in interviews.

How long is the cohort?

Eight core weeks of Saturday and Sunday live sessions, plus a bonus week.

When does it start?

Saturday, 14 November 2026.
