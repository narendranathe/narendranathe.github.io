# HLD Cohort 2.0 mirror PRD

## Objective
Replace the unused DE Lab with an isolated authorized HLD cohort content mirror at /hld-cohort/. No payment or enrollment backend.

## Functional acceptance criteria
- Accessible navigation, mobile menu, skip link and scroll indicator.
- Nine expandable curriculum weeks; client-side topic search; expand and collapse controls.
- Fourteen design problems with client-side search.
- Four free simulator links, sample class previews, instructor profile, student feedback, FAQ and pricing information.
- External links open the original website; no fake payment or login.
- No /de-lab/ files, no obsolete DE Lab CI or PRD.

## Limitations
Original simulator applications remain hosted by the source website, not replicated in this static site. This version is an improved public content mirror, not a pixel-perfect screenshot reproduction. Some syllabus bullets and testimonial quotes are abbreviated; all private cohort resources are out of scope.

## Test plan
Check mobile and desktop breakpoints, anchor navigation, search and expand/collapse, FAQ keyboard operation, simulator links, no payment forms, no stale DE Lab assets, deployment after merge.
