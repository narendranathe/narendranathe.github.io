Why extract the engine

The most important design decision was not adding more product surface. It was taking the tailoring logic out of the larger application stack so it could stand on its own and support multiple delivery surfaces honestly.

  • It made the broader system easier to reason about.
  • It created a reusable unit with a clear responsibility.
  • It gave recruiters and engineering leaders a clean way to inspect one part of the platform in depth.

What the engine does

  • Profile extraction from resume artifacts.
  • ATS gap analysis against a job description.
  • Tailored resume output with disciplined claim handling.
  • Multiple interfaces over one core pipeline.
